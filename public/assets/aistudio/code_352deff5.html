ABOUT YOUR ADVOCATE

Meet Jasmeen Williams, LPN, BS-HHS

Founder & Principal Care Navigator

Jasmeen Williams founded Southern Well Senior Concierge on a simple conviction: older adults deserve to navigate aging with dignity, clarity, and unhurried attention—and their families deserve a trustworthy advocate who treats every detail as if it were for her own loved ones.

A South Mississippi Health & Human Services leader with over eight years of experience as a Licensed Practical Nurse (LPN) and a Bachelor of Science in Health & Human Services (BS-HHS), Jasmeen has dedicated her career to patient advocacy, care coordination, and elevating quality, access, and experience across diverse populations. Serving families across Hattiesburg, the Pine Belt, and the Gulfport / Mississippi Gulf Coast, she combines clinical fluency with warm, meticulous organization—working alongside local physicians, Licensed Clinical Social Workers (LCSWs), and elder law partners so nothing falls through the cracks.

CLARITY & COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Southern Well Senior Concierge a home health or medical agency? keyboard_arrow_down
No. Although founded by an experienced Licensed Practical Nurse (LPN, BS-HHS), Southern Well operates strictly as a non-clinical care navigation and advocacy service. We do not dispense medications, perform skilled nursing assessments, or provide medical or clinical mental health treatment. Instead, we handle the logistical, organizational, and communication details—and coordinate warm referrals to licensed physicians, home health agencies, and Licensed Clinical Social Workers (LCSWs) whenever clinical care is needed.
How do you help with medications if your service is non-clinical? keyboard_arrow_down
We provide organizational medication oversight: keeping an up-to-date master medication list, checking expiration dates, helping organize weekly pillboxes strictly according to existing prescription bottle labels, flagging potential refill gaps or duplicate prescriptions for your pharmacist or doctor to review, and coordinating timely pharmacy refills.
Which areas in South Mississippi do you serve? keyboard_arrow_down
We serve families across two primary hubs: Hattiesburg & the Pine Belt (including Laurel, Petal, and Oak Grove) and Gulfport & the Mississippi Gulf Coast (including Biloxi, Long Beach, and Ocean Springs).
How do we get started? keyboard_arrow_down
Begin with a complimentary 30-minute phone, video, or in-person family consultation. We will listen to your current concerns, answer your questions, and recommend the right starting tier.
