## Self-Efficacy for Caregiving (CSES-8)

How sure are you that you can keep up your own activities and also respond to caregiving situations? For each question, select how sure you are that you can do the task regularly at the present time. 1 = not at all confident, 10 = totally confident.

Scale score (mean of answered items)

**Not scored**

Scoring: each item's score is the number selected. The scale score is the mean of the eight items. If more than two items are missing, the scale is not scored. A higher number means higher self-efficacy. For reference, the 47 caregivers in the original study averaged 5.88 (SD 1.90). On paper, if two consecutive numbers are circled, code the lower one; if they are not consecutive, do not score the item. This page accepts one number per item.
